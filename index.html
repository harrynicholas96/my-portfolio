Hey, I'm Harry

Thanks for stopping by to explore my work.

I'm a product designer driven by human-centred thinking, combining UX/UI expertise with a 7-year foundation in digital product development. My background in front-end development gives me a strong understanding of how things are built — and how to design experiences that are both intuitive and feasible.

While I care deeply about clean, thoughtful interfaces, I see design as more than just how something looks on a screen. For me, it's about solving real problems, reducing friction, and creating experiences that genuinely help people achieve what they set out to do. I focus on crafting accessible, user-centered products that balance business goals with meaningful outcomes.

I'm currently based in Swindon - well-connected to Bristol, Oxfordshire, and just an hour from London by train.
